# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Asuka Agent primarily serves its owner and developers or researchers studying long-running chat agents. They use the console to inspect conversations, IM ingestion, memory, thoughts, schedules, model configuration, and audit evidence without losing sight of the underlying event flow.

## Product Purpose

Asuka Agent makes a persistent chat agent observable, reviewable, and safe to evolve. Success means the agent maintains continuity across channels, distinguishes people and evidence correctly, acts only within explicit policy boundaries, and gives its owner enough visibility to correct mistakes before autonomy expands.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, trustworthy, and companionable. Copy should be calm and precise while retaining the warmth of a personal agent rather than sounding like an enterprise operations suite.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic enterprise administration screens, decorative AI SaaS dashboards, opaque “magic” controls, and interfaces that overstate autonomy or certainty. Do not trade legibility for glass effects, excessive motion, tiny labels, or ornamental metrics.

## Design Principles

1. Make state and provenance visible before exposing more autonomy.
2. Keep common workflows familiar; reserve personality for language and small details.
3. Present safe defaults, explicit consequences, and actionable recovery paths.
4. Preserve a clear distinction between people, conversations, evidence, and model inference.
5. Prefer dense but readable operational context over decorative dashboard elements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, semantic labels and status announcements, and reduced-motion preferences. Never rely on color alone to communicate connection, validation, or error state.
